Drag on a row to add an action, including under an existing one. Drag a block to move it, its right edge to resize it, or the dots on its wind-up and wind-down lines to change them. Drag ≡ to reorder rows. Drag the red flag on the ruler to move the now marker. Shortcuts: Delete removes the selected event, Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste an event, Ctrl+S downloads, Ctrl+O uploads, Ctrl and plus or minus zooms the timeline.
