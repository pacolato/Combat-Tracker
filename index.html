Combat Tracker

How to use

  • Drag horizontally across a row to add an event, even directly under another one.
  • Drag an event to move it (to another row too), or drag its right edge to resize it.
  • Click an event to edit its title, description, colour and times in the pop-up.
  • Drag the dot at the end of a wind-up or wind-down line to change it.
  • Drag ≡ to reorder rows. Use the + at the end of the ruler to add 15 seconds to the timeline.
  • Drag the red flag on the ruler to move the now marker.
  • Times are capped at 3600 seconds. Your work is saved on this device automatically.
  • Use the buttons at the top right to upload or download a timeline.
  • The Reset button will clear the entire timeline and restore defaults.

Shortcuts
